New Knee Society Knee Scoring System (2011) (KSS-2011)

Updated version of the classic KSS with separate patient-reported and clinician-assessed components. Includes expectation and satisfaction domains.

  • 9 questions
  • 10-15 min
  • Score 0 to 100, higher is better
  • Well Validated
All scores

About the KSS-2011

Who it is for

Patients undergoing TKA where comprehensive assessment including clinical examination is needed.

What it measures

Objective knee examination, patient symptoms, satisfaction with surgery, and functional activities including advanced activities.

Sections

  • Objective Knee Score
  • Symptoms (3 items)
  • Patient Satisfaction (5 items)
  • Functional Activities (19 items)
  • Expectations

Reading your score

Multiple subscores, each 0-100. Higher = better for all subscales.

Strengths

  • Comprehensive modern assessment
  • Includes patient satisfaction
  • Separates PROM from clinical exam
  • Free to use

Limitations

  • More complex than original KSS
  • Longer administration time
  • Requires trained examiner for objective section

Source

Scuderi GR, et al. The new Knee Society Knee Scoring System. Clin Orthop Relat Res. 2012;470(1):3-19. Read the paper
