Knee Injury and Osteoarthritis Outcome Score (KOOS)

Comprehensive patient-reported outcome measure developed for younger, more active patients with knee injury who are at increased risk of developing OA. Includes WOMAC items as subscales.

  • 42 questions
  • 10-15 min
  • Score 0 to 100, higher is better
  • Gold Standard
All scores

About the KOOS

Who it is for

Patients with knee injuries (ACL, meniscus, cartilage) and those with early to moderate OA. Ideal for younger, active patients where sports function matters.

What it measures

Five separately scored subscales: Pain, other Symptoms, Function in daily living (ADL), Function in sport/recreation (Sport/Rec), and knee-related Quality of life (QOL). Provides multidimensional assessment.

Sections

  • Pain (9 items)
  • Symptoms (7 items)
  • ADL (17 items)
  • Sport/Rec (5 items)
  • QOL (4 items)

Reading your score

Each subscale scored 0-100, where 0 = extreme problems, 100 = no problems. Subscales reported separately, not summed.

Strengths

  • Comprehensive coverage of all relevant domains
  • Separate subscale scores allow targeted assessment
  • Excellent psychometric properties across all subscales
  • Free to use - no license required
  • WOMAC embedded for OA comparison
  • Widely translated and culturally adapted

Limitations

  • Length may affect compliance in clinical practice
  • Sport/Rec subscale less relevant for sedentary patients
  • Some redundancy with comprehensive item set
  • No single summary score

Source

Roos EM, et al. Knee injury and Osteoarthritis Outcome Score (KOOS). Scand J Med Sci Sports. 1998;8(6):439-448. Read the paper
