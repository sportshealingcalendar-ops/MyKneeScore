International Knee Documentation Committee Subjective Knee Evaluation Form (IKDC)

Developed by an international committee to evaluate symptoms, function, and sports activities in patients with various knee conditions. The gold standard for ligament injury assessment.

  • 18 questions
  • 5-8 min
  • Score 0 to 100, higher is better
  • Gold Standard

About the IKDC

Who it is for

Designed for patients with ligamentous injuries, meniscal pathology, or articular cartilage lesions who are active or wish to return to activity. Best suited for younger, more active individuals.

What it measures

The IKDC measures symptoms (pain, swelling, locking, giving way), sports activities capability, and current knee function. It provides a single overall score reflecting the patient's perceived knee health.

Sections

  • Symptoms
  • Sports Activities
  • Function

Reading your score

Higher scores represent higher levels of function and fewer symptoms. Scores can be categorized: Normal (≥90), Near Normal (80-89), Abnormal (70-79), Severely Abnormal (<70).

Strengths

  • Gold standard for ligament injuries with extensive validation
  • Excellent responsiveness to detect treatment effects
  • Single summary score simplifies interpretation
  • Free to use without license
  • Widely used enabling cross-study comparison

Limitations

  • Ceiling effects in less active populations
  • Some items may not apply to all patients
  • Sport-focused items less relevant for sedentary patients
  • Does not separate pain from function subscales

Source

Irrgang JJ, et al. Development of a patient-reported measure of function of the knee. J Bone Joint Surg Am. 2001;83(8):1140-1148. Read the paper
