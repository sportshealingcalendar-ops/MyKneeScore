Pediatric IKDC Subjective Knee Evaluation Form (Pedi-IKDC)

Pediatric-specific version of IKDC validated for children and adolescents ages 10-18 with various knee conditions.

  • 18 questions
  • 5-8 min
  • Score 0 to 100, higher is better
  • Well Validated
All scores

About the Pedi-IKDC

Who it is for

Children and adolescents (10-18 years) with knee injuries or conditions requiring outcome assessment.

What it measures

Knee symptoms, sports function, and daily activities adapted for pediatric language and activities.

Sections

  • Symptoms
  • Sports Activities
  • Function

Reading your score

0-100 scale, higher = better function. Similar interpretation to adult IKDC.

Strengths

  • Age-appropriate language and activities
  • Validated for pediatric population
  • Based on proven IKDC
  • Free to use

Limitations

  • Limited to pediatric population (10-18)
  • May require parental assistance for younger children
  • Cannot compare directly to adult IKDC

Source

Kocher MS, et al. Reliability, validity, and responsiveness of the pediatric IKDC subjective knee evaluation form. Am J Sports Med. 2011;39(5):933-939. Read the paper
