mykneescore Knee Sleep Score - KS5 (KS5)

UNVALIDATED. A brief 5-item measure of how knee problems affect sleep. Developed to address the under-assessment of sleep in knee surgery trials (only 0.4% of 501 RCTs used a sleep-specific PROM).

  • 5 questions
  • 1 min
  • Score 0 to 100, higher is better
  • Unvalidated

This score is unvalidated. Treat the result as a guide only.

All scores

About the KS5

Who it is for

Patients before and after knee surgery, especially knee replacement, where sleep disturbance is linked to pain, function and satisfaction.

What it measures

How often knee pain stops you falling asleep, wakes you at night or when turning in bed, morning knee stiffness, and needing to sleep with the knee bent (Not at all / Some days / Most days / Some pain every day / Severe pain every day).

Sections

  • Single composite score

Reading your score

0-100 score (sum of 5 items scored 0-4 / 20 x 100). Higher = better sleep. No validated thresholds - interpret with caution.

Strengths

  • Very brief (about 1 minute)
  • Knee-specific sleep focus not covered by KOOS, WOMAC, OKS or KSS
  • Can be added alongside any knee score

Limitations

  • UNVALIDATED - no published reliability, validity or responsiveness data
  • Not a substitute for validated sleep measures such as the PSQI
  • No MCID or normative data

Source
