About the PK7

Who it is for

Clinicians and researchers who want domain-level visibility with customizable weighting. Suitable for detailed assessment where clinician prioritization of domains is valuable.

What it measures

Seven key knee domains: pain, sleep disturbance, swelling, instability/giving way, stiffness/movement restriction, stair management, and overall function/mobility.

Sections

  • Pain
  • Sleep
  • Swelling
  • Instability
  • Stiffness
  • Stairs
  • Function

Reading your score

0-100 score. Each domain normalized to 0-100, weighted sum computed. Bands: Excellent (90-100), Good (75-89), Moderate (50-74), Fair (25-49), Poor (0-24).

Strengths

  • Domain-level breakdown with individual tracking
  • Clinician-adjustable weights for personalized assessment
  • Mixed native scales normalized to common 0-100
  • Oura-style weight normalization
  • Brief completion despite comprehensive coverage
  • Handles partial responses via weight renormalization

Limitations

  • Custom weights may reduce cross-study comparability
  • Newer instrument with emerging validation
  • Mixed scales may be unfamiliar to some respondents

Source

mykneescore development team. 2025. Formal external validation in progress.
