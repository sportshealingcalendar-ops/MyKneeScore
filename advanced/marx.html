Marx Activity Rating Scale (Marx)

Brief activity scale focusing on high-demand knee activities. Complements functional scores by quantifying activity level.

  • 4 questions
  • 1-2 min
  • Score 0 to 16, higher is better
  • Well Validated
All scores

About the Marx

Who it is for

Athletes and active individuals where high-demand activities are relevant. Documents activity exposure.

What it measures

Frequency of participation in four high-demand activities: running, cutting, decelerating, and pivoting. Each rated from 0 (never) to 4 (4+ times/week).

Sections

  • Running
  • Cutting
  • Deceleration
  • Pivoting

Reading your score

Sum of 4 items (0-4 each). Higher = more frequent high-demand activity.

Strengths

  • Very brief
  • Quantifies high-demand activity frequency
  • Free to use
  • Complements functional scores

Limitations

  • Limited to high-demand activities
  • Floor effects in sedentary patients
  • Does not capture recreational activities

Source

Marx RG, et al. Development and evaluation of an activity rating scale for disorders of the knee. Am J Sports Med. 2001;29(2):213-218. Read the paper
