About the SK11

Who it is for

Patients of all ages with any knee condition. Ideal for busy clinics, screening, and digital pathways requiring brief assessment. Awaiting formal validation.

What it measures

Comprehensive knee function across comfort, stiffness, swelling, stability, walking, stairs, kneeling/squatting, mechanical symptoms, activity level, overall function, and sleep impact.

Sections

  • Single composite score

Reading your score

0-100 score. Bands: Excellent (90-100), Good (75-89), Moderate (50-74), Fair (25-49), Poor (0-24). Score = round(10 × Σ(q_i × w_i)) with renormalized weights.

Strengths

  • Very brief completion time (2-3 minutes)
  • Fixed weights ensure consistency
  • Handles partial responses via weight renormalization
  • All items phrased consistently (10=best)
  • Single composite score for easy interpretation
  • Digital-first design with real-time scoring

Limitations

  • No subscale analysis available
  • Fixed weights may not suit all research contexts
  • Newer instrument with emerging validation

Source

mykneescore development team. 2025. Formal external validation in progress.
