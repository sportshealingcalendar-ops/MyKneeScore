Banff Patella Instability Instrument (BPII)

Comprehensive quality of life measure designed specifically for patients with patellar instability. Captures physical and psychological impact.

  • 23 questions
  • 10-15 min
  • Score 0 to 100, higher is better
  • Validated
All scores

About the BPII

Who it is for

Patients with patellar instability, recurrent dislocation, or post-stabilization surgery requiring comprehensive QOL assessment.

What it measures

QOL impact of patellar instability across physical, functional, lifestyle, emotional, and social domains.

Sections

  • Physical Symptoms (6 items)
  • Work/Recreation/Sports (9 items)
  • Lifestyle Changes (7 items)
  • Emotional Health (5 items)
  • Social Concerns (5 items)

Reading your score

0 = worst, 100 = best QOL. Average of VAS items × 100.

Strengths

  • Comprehensive instability-specific QOL
  • Captures psychological impact
  • Good psychometric properties
  • Free to use

Limitations

  • Length may affect compliance
  • Specific to instability only
  • Relatively newer measure

Source

Hiemstra LA, et al. Development of the Banff Patella Instability Instrument. Clin J Sport Med. 2013;23(6):417-423. Read the paper
