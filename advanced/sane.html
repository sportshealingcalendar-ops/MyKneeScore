Single Assessment Numeric Evaluation (SANE)

Single-item global knee rating. Patient rates knee function as percentage of normal. Useful for quick assessment and correlation with longer measures.

  • 1 question
  • <1 min
  • Score 0 to 100, higher is better
  • Validated
All scores

About the SANE

Who it is for

Any patient where a quick global assessment is needed. Useful for screening or supplementing detailed measures.

What it measures

Patient's global assessment of knee function as a percentage of normal (100% = completely normal knee).

Sections

  • Global Assessment

Reading your score

0-100%, patient's self-assessment of knee as percentage of normal.

Strengths

  • Extremely brief
  • Easy to understand
  • Correlates well with detailed scores
  • Free to use
  • No calculation needed

Limitations

  • Single item - no detail
  • Subjective interpretation of 'normal'
  • Not suitable as primary research outcome

Source

Williams GN, et al. Comparison of the Single Assessment Numeric Evaluation method and two shoulder rating scales. Am J Sports Med. 1999;27(2):214-221. Read the paper
