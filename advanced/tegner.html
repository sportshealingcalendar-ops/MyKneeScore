Tegner Activity Scale (Tegner)

Single-item scale grading work and sports activities on a scale of 0-10. Essential companion to Lysholm score for documenting activity level changes.

  • 1 question
  • 1 min
  • Score 0 to 10, higher is better
  • Well Validated
All scores

About the Tegner

Who it is for

Any patient where activity level documentation is needed. Essential for ACL and sports knee research to document pre-injury, current, and goal activity levels.

What it measures

Current activity level on a numeric scale from 0 (disability pension) to 10 (competitive soccer at national/international level). Captures both work and sports activities.

Sections

  • Activity Level

Reading your score

0 = disability/sick leave, 1-4 = work/light activities, 5-6 = recreational sports, 7-9 = competitive sports, 10 = national/international soccer.

Strengths

  • Extremely brief - single item
  • Easy to understand and complete
  • Essential for documenting activity changes
  • Free to use
  • Widely understood activity benchmarks

Limitations

  • Single item limits detail
  • Activity definitions may be culturally specific
  • Not a comprehensive functional measure
  • Some overlap between activity levels

Source

Tegner Y, Lysholm J. Rating systems in the evaluation of knee ligament injuries. Clin Orthop Relat Res. 1985;(198):43-49. PubMed
