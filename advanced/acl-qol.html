ACL Quality of Life Questionnaire (ACL-QOL)

Disease-specific quality of life measure designed specifically for ACL-deficient patients. Captures the unique impact of ACL injury on daily life.

  • 32 questions
  • 10-12 min
  • Score 0 to 100, higher is better
  • Well Validated
All scores

About the ACL-QOL

Who it is for

Patients with ACL deficiency (torn ACL) or post-ACL reconstruction. Focus on quality of life impact rather than just symptoms.

What it measures

Six domains assessing the comprehensive impact of ACL deficiency on symptoms, physical function, work, sports, lifestyle, and emotional wellbeing.

Sections

  • Symptoms (5 items)
  • Physical Complaints (5 items)
  • Work Concerns (4 items)
  • Recreation/Sports (12 items)
  • Lifestyle (4 items)
  • Social/Emotional (2 items)

Reading your score

Higher scores indicate better quality of life. Average of all items on 100mm VAS.

Strengths

  • Only ACL-specific QOL measure
  • Comprehensive domain coverage
  • Excellent responsiveness
  • Free to use

Limitations

  • Specific to ACL population only
  • Length may affect compliance
  • Not suitable for acute injury phase
  • Limited to ACL pathology

Source

Mohtadi N. Development and validation of the quality of life outcome measure (questionnaire) for chronic anterior cruciate ligament deficiency. Am J Sports Med. 1998;26(3):350-359. Read the paper
