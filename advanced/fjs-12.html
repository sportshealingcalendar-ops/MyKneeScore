Forgotten Joint Score (FJS-12)

Measures patient's ability to 'forget' their artificial joint in daily life. Captures joint awareness in high-functioning patients where traditional PROMs show ceiling effects.

  • 12 questions
  • 3-5 min
  • Score 0 to 100, higher is better
  • Well Validated
All scores

About the FJS-12

Who it is for

Post-arthroplasty patients, especially for detecting differences in high-functioning patients and comparing implant performance.

What it measures

How often patients are aware of their replaced joint during various daily activities. Unique concept capturing joint awareness rather than function.

Sections

  • Joint Awareness

Reading your score

0 = always aware of joint, 100 = never aware (joint 'forgotten'). Higher = better.

Strengths

  • Minimal ceiling effects
  • Unique concept not captured by other scores
  • Brief administration
  • Free to use
  • Excellent for high-functioning patients

Limitations

  • May show floor effects in poorly-functioning patients
  • Specific concept may not suit all research questions
  • Not suitable pre-operatively

Source

Behrend H, et al. The 'Forgotten Joint' as the ultimate goal in joint arthroplasty: validation of a new patient-reported outcome measure. J Arthroplasty. 2012;27(3):430-436.e1. Read the paper
