Kujala Anterior Knee Pain Scale (Kujala/AKPS)

Most widely used patellofemoral-specific outcome measure for anterior knee pain and patellofemoral disorders.

  • 13 questions
  • 5-7 min
  • Score 0 to 100, higher is better
  • Well Validated
All scores

About the Kujala/AKPS

Who it is for

Patients with anterior knee pain, patellofemoral pain syndrome, or chondromalacia patellae. All ages including adolescents.

What it measures

Thirteen items covering functional limitations and symptoms specific to patellofemoral pain. Weighted scoring emphasizes key functional activities.

Sections

  • Limp
  • Weight-bearing
  • Walking
  • Stairs
  • Squatting
  • Running
  • Jumping
  • Prolonged sitting
  • Pain
  • Swelling
  • Patellar subluxation
  • Atrophy
  • Flexion deficiency

Reading your score

0 = worst, 100 = no disability. Weighted sum of 13 items.

Strengths

  • Most widely used PF pain score
  • Well validated across populations
  • Free to use
  • Covers key PF-specific activities

Limitations

  • Some ceiling effects in mild cases
  • Limited validation in surgical populations
  • Does not capture instability well
  • Scoring can be complex

Source

Kujala UM, et al. Scoring of patellofemoral disorders. Arthroscopy. 1993;9(2):159-163. Read the paper
